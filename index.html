How the calculation works

1 metric ton = 1,000 kg. Delivered cost = purchase cost + full-load freight + other costs. Financing uses simple interest on that total: delivered cost × annual rate × days ÷ 365. Break-even includes financing; margin is profit divided by sales revenue. These figures exclude GST, defaults, weight loss and any costs you have not entered. Financing is an estimate; actual lender charges may differ.
